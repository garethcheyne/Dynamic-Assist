# Privacy policy

Both stores want a **URL**, not text: store.json → `listing.privacyPolicyUrl`,
https://www.err403.com/privacy-policy/chrome-extensions (the policy for every
err403 extension).

What Dynamic Assist itself reads, keeps and shares is on the website, which is
where it's kept up to date:

- The extension and the companion app: https://dynamicassist.err403.com/privacy/
- The website (visits, feedback, emails): https://dynamicassist.err403.com/privacy/website/

When the extension starts reading, keeping or sending something new, change the
website's page (`src/content/privacy.mdx` in the website repo), then
`data-usage.md` and `permissions.md` here, before the release goes to the stores.
