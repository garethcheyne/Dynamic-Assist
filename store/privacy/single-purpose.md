# Single purpose

Chrome Web Store → Privacy → **Single purpose description**, and Edge Partner
Center → Privacy → **Purpose**. One or two sentences:
an extension must have a single, narrow purpose that users can understand.

A side panel of administrator and developer tools for Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Business Central and model-driven apps), the Power Apps maker portal and Power Automate: it shows what's behind the page you're on (its records, fields, IDs, permissions and environment) and takes you to related admin and developer pages.
