Dynamic Assist puts the everyday tools that administrators and developers need for Microsoft Business Central, Dynamics 365, Power Apps and Power Automate into one side panel. Open one of those sites and the panel shows the tools for it; switch tabs and it follows. Every tool is described on the website: https://dynamicassist.err403.com/docs/

BUSINESS CENTRAL
• Page details, table IDs, the owning app, and every field with its value, ready to copy
• Field names on the page, all fields of the record, and page tools such as blur for screenshots
• Go to any page, table, report or query by number
• Query builder: query any table you can read, with filters, related tables or SQL, and export to Excel, CSV or JSON or turn it into AL or an API request (needs the free Dynamic Assist Companion app)

DYNAMICS 365 APPS
• Record details and all columns, logical names on forms, god mode, fill required fields and clone record
• Errors on the page with the server's own message, and debug switches
• Access and Compare: a user's privileges and roles, and two users side by side
• Query builder with FetchXML and SQL, saved queries, and the coupled Business Central records

POWER APPS AND POWER AUTOMATE
• The environment and solution IDs, and shortcuts to the maker and admin pages
• Turn many cloud flows on or off at once, see failed runs, and find connection references and environment variables that need attention

EVERYWHERE
• Pinned and recent environments, a Go anywhere menu, a right-click menu, a pop-out window, and light and dark themes

WHAT IT ACCESSES
Dynamic Assist works only on Business Central, Dynamics 365, Power Apps and Power Automate sites. It reads the page you're on and calls that service's own API as you, with your existing sign-in and permissions. It never reads or stores passwords or tokens, and it changes data only when you click a tool that does. Nothing is sent to any other server, there is no account and no tracking, and your history and settings stay in your browser. Links to the Dynamic Assist website open in a new tab only when you click them.
